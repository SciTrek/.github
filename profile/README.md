## About SciTrek

[SciTrek](https://scitrek.chem.ucsb.edu/) is UC Santa Barbara's largest science education outreach program, run through the Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry since 2010. UCSB student mentors partner with elementary (2nd–6th grade) and secondary (7th–12th grade) classrooms to run multi-day, hands-on science investigations — from experimental design through poster presentations — aligned with Next Generation Science Standards.

This organization hosts the open-source tools built by and for the SciTrek program: the software that schedules our volunteer mentors and the modules that bring bioinformatics into the curriculum.
